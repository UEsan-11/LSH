BIOGRAPHY
이수혁（李洙赫），1988年5月31日生於韓國京畿道果川市。

2006年以設計師鄭旭俊（Lone Costume）服裝秀模特兒身分出道，與 G-Dragon、Kim Woo-bin 同為同期模特兒。憑藉 184cm 的高挑身形與冷峻眼神，迅速成為韓國頂尖男模，並登上巴黎、倫敦等國際時裝週。

2010年11月4日以電影《依帕內瑪少年》正式演員出道，2011年以 KBS 校園劇《White Christmas》廣受矚目，2015年在 MBC 奇幻劇《夜行書生》飾演吸血鬼反派「鬼」，獲得廣大迴響，並奪得 MBC 演技大賞男子新人獎。

2021年《某一天滅亡來到我家門前》、2022年《還有明天》使他的海外知名度大幅攀升。2024年主演《于氏王后》，帝王氣場備受讚譽。2025年加入新公司 Saram Entertainment，展開全新篇章。
Lee Soo-hyuk was born on May 31, 1988, in Gwacheon, Gyeonggi-do, South Korea.

He debuted as a model in 2006 at designer Jung Wook-jun's runway show, alongside G-Dragon and Kim Woo-bin. His 184cm frame and striking features quickly established him as one of Korea's top male models, taking him to international runways in Paris and London.

He made his official acting debut on November 4, 2010, with the film Boy from Ipanema. In 2011, KBS drama White Christmas brought him wider recognition. His breakthrough came in 2015 with MBC's Scholar Who Walks the Night, where his portrayal of the vampire antagonist "Gwi" earned him a Best New Actor award.

International recognition followed with Doom at Your Service (2021) and Tomorrow (2022). After signing with Saram Entertainment in 2025, he continues to expand his artistic range.
